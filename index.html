About Me

I am completing my Ph.D. in Computer Science at ETH Zurich, advised by Mrinmaya Sachan and Antoine Bosselut. Previously, I completed my M.Phil. at CUHK and my B.Eng. at HUST.

Research

My research asks how language models ground language in perceptual and contextual evidence, reason over that evidence, and remain reliable when evidence or tasks change. I combine behavioral diagnostics with mechanistic analysis to identify shortcut-driven failures, then develop training- and inference-time interventions that improve evidence use and interpretability. Multimodal settings provide a revealing testbed because they expose the interface between perception, language, and reasoning.
